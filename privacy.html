Effect date: 1st March, 2026Last Updated: 13th September, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TABI collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, dashboard, APIs, messaging products, integrations, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, business details, account credentials, billing information, support requests, and any information you submit through the Service.

We also collect technical and usage information such as IP address, device and browser details, log data, pages visited, feature usage, API activity, and cookies or similar technologies.

When you use TABI to communicate with customers, you may provide contact details, messages, media, templates, campaign data, and other customer information. You remain responsible for obtaining the permissions and legal basis required to process that data. TABI processes Customer Data only as needed to provide, secure, and improve the Service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain the Service; authenticate users; process subscriptions; communicate with you; provide support; monitor security; prevent fraud and abuse; analyse product performance; and comply with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Service, including hosting, payment, analytics, support, and security providers. We may also disclose information where required by law, to enforce our agreements, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of TABI, our users, or others.

Use of WhatsApp functionality is also subject to the terms and policies of WhatsApp and Meta. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or platform decisions of third parties, including message delivery, account approval, or policy enforcement.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain business records. You may request deletion of your account data, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No system is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your account credentials, API keys, and integrations confidential.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version and update the “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

12. Contact Us

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hi@tabi.com.
